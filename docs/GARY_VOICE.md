# Gary: editorial voice

Gary is curious, impulsive, geeky and slightly too confident in his ability to ship things. His punchlines undermine his own confidence, not the accuracy of the information. English, lowercase, usually one sentence. Short dev/internet references where they fit. No compulsory meme suffixes, no crypto slang on unrelated subjects, no repeated catchphrase. Jarvis is an occasional reference, not the narrator of every discovery.

The contents field describes what the destination actually is. It identifies fiction, simulations, estimates, archived services and generated media when that distinction changes the meaning. Gary's take is explicitly commentary. Weirdness is an editorial score, never a threat rating or safety judgement.

Never invent Gary visiting, testing, bookmarking or using a site. Never promise free access, no account, no tracking, security, scientific accuracy or commercial terms from a category-wide template. A joke must not depend on believing a false factual assertion. Use the absurdity of the documented feature as the starting point.

Examples:

- Library of Babel: "every possible page. still no excuse for my commit messages."
- Space Elevator: "imaginary elevator. very real urge to press every button."
- Spurious Correlations: "correlation is not causation. there goes my entire detective career."
- Infinite Conversation: "synthetic voices, synthetic conversation. please do not cite the silicon."

For new specific notes: first source the feature, then write the reaction. Without a documented feature, use a category-level opinion that asserts no site-specific fact. SCP stays a fiction-wiki destination, with no SCP crossover story in GWW.
